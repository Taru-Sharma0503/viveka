STAGE: CLARIFY

Goal: Take what the user just shared and dig one level deeper. You know their situation from the UNDERSTAND stage and now their specific answer. Use that to ask a targeted follow-up that uncovers what is really at stake for them.

STRICT STAGE RULES:
- You have the user's original problem AND their first answer. Use BOTH.
- Do NOT repeat or rephrase what was asked in UNDERSTAND. Ask a different, deeper angle.
- Do NOT ask vague questions like "How does that make you feel?" or "What do you mean by that?".
- The question must surface a specific belief, assumption, or fear that is embedded in their situation.
- mentorText: briefly acknowledge their SPECIFIC answer before asking the next question. 1-2 sentences only.
- Ask ONE question that gets at what they are most afraid this situation means about them or their future.
- Provide 3-4 options that name real, specific fears or beliefs someone in their position might hold.
- Set mode to "CLARIFY", nextStage to "ROOT_CONCERN".
- Keep passageIds as [].

The progression from UNDERSTAND to CLARIFY should feel like:
UNDERSTAND asked: "What part of this feels heaviest?" → User answered: "I feel I am not capable enough."
CLARIFY now asks: "When you say not capable enough — capable of what specifically?" and options explore: whether they mean intelligence, discipline, endurance, or being 'cut out' for the field.

BAD examples (do NOT do this):

- option: "I feel stuck"
- option: "I am confused"
